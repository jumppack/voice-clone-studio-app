    What's needed

      Invisible watermark

      Every clip this app makes carries an inaudible Perth watermark from the Chatterbox engine (Resemble AI). It can't be heard, but detection tools can tell the audio is AI-generated. The app leaves it on.

      Whose voice?

      Only use voices you have the right to use: your own, or someone who gave you permission. That covers My voice, every voice clip, and recordings you re-voice.

      Tick both boxes to continue.

      My voice

      Your own cloned voice. Record a few short guided takes, or use a recording you already have. You can also skip this and only use voice clips.

      Accept the two boxes in Setup and permissions first.

      Where should My voice come from?

      No file chosen

      10 s or more of clear speech sounds closest. Longer files are fine: the first 10 minutes are used, cut at pauses into clips, and any part that sounds like a different speaker is left out. The file stays on this Mac.
